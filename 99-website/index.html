A few questions.

Can I add more pages later?

Yes — the $99 site is built the same way as our full packages, so it upgrades cleanly into the Grow package instead of getting rebuilt from scratch.

What if I don't like the layout?

You see it before anything's final and get one round of edits included. Big design changes are outside the $99 scope — that's what the full packages are for.

Do you host it? Do I need a domain?

Hosting's on us, on a shared platform — decent, dependable load speed for a one-page site, though we won't promise a specific speed score. A domain is the one thing you buy yourself, from any registrar; we'll walk you through pointing it at the site.

Is this a subscription?

No. $99 once for the build. The site is yours, hosted by us — no monthly platform fee.
